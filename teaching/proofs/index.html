Proofs from THE BOOK

Graduate/undergraduate course · PUC-Rio, March–June 2024 · Tuesdays and Thursdays 9–11am, room 863, Edifício Leme

The aim of the course is to enjoy the beauty of mathematical proofs in different areas: number theory, algebra, analysis and beyond. The main source of inspiration is the book by M. Aigner and G. Ziegler, Proofs from THE BOOK, complemented by other interesting mathematical stories. Students choose their favorite topic and give a presentation on it.

Materials

Books: M. Aigner, G. Ziegler, Proofs from the BOOK; S. Tabachnikov, D. Fuchs, Mathematical Omnibus: Thirty Lectures on Classic Mathematics.
